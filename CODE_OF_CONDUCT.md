# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the LifeFlow AI project a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Giving helpful feedback.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Collaborating professionally.
- Helping create a positive community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks.
- Hate speech.
- Spam or abusive language.
- Sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a private discussion with the project maintainer.
